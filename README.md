# Thomas Johnson

### Clinical biomechanics · Sensor data · Applied machine learning

I’m a Stockholm-based clinician specialised in gait analysis and applied biomechanics, I also have a background in physiology and human movement science, currently completing an **MSc in Sports Technology at KTH**.

I combine clinical experience with programming and research to explore how we can turn measurements of the human body into useful information for healthcare, rehabilitation and sport. My interests include **sensor signal processing, measurement validation, computer vision and machine learning**.

[Portfolio](https://tdjohnson1987.github.io/) · [GitHub projects](https://github.com/tdjohnson1987?tab=repositories)

## What I bring

- **Clinical understanding:** experience in gait assessment, rehabilitation and orthotic management across adult, paediatric and neurological care.
- **Measurement and research:** plantar-pressure research at KTH’s MoveAbility Lab, alongside work with wearable sensors and laboratory movement-analysis systems.
- **Technical development:** Python workflows for data acquisition, signal processing, visualisation and machine learning, plus mobile application development.
- **Communication:** clinical teaching and education, with experience working across healthcare, research and industry.

## Selected projects
